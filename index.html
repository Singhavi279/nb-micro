Platform & Institutional Excellence

SEBI/RBI/IRDAI-regulated platforms, banks, wealth firms and fintechs serving Indian retail investors.

✓ Investor Trust & Transparency Platform of the Year
Who Can Apply

SEBI/RBI/IRDAI-regulated or otherwise eligible investment, brokerage, bank, wealth, fintech or financial-services platforms serving Indian retail investors.

✓ Digital Brokerage & Trading Platform of the Year – Investor Experience
Who Can Apply

Registered stock brokers and digital brokerage/trading platforms serving Indian retail investors.

✓ Mutual Fund House of the Year – Investor Education
Who Can Apply

AMFI-member / SEBI-registered asset management companies and mutual fund houses operating in India.

✓ Fixed Income & Bonds Platform of the Year – Investor Education
Who Can Apply

Regulated bond/fixed-income platforms, brokerages, banks, AMCs and wealth platforms with a material retail fixed-income proposition.

✓ Wealth Management Platform of the Year – Investor Education & Client Experience
Who Can Apply

Wealth managers, private banks, portfolio managers, digital wealth platforms and advisory firms with an India retail/affluent proposition.

✓ Bank of the Year – Financial Literacy & Investor Engagement
Who Can Apply

Scheduled commercial banks, small finance banks and eligible banking institutions with documented financial literacy/investor education initiatives.

✓ Wealth Advisory Firm of the Year – Investor Education & Client Engagement
Who Can Apply

SEBI-registered investment advisers/portfolio managers and eligible wealth/advisory firms with a documented client-education practice.

✓ Research & Market Intelligence Platform of the Year – Investor Empowerment
Who Can Apply

Research, analytics, screening, market-intelligence and investor-information platforms serving retail investors in India.

✓ Digital Wealth Advisory Platform of the Year
Who Can Apply

Digital wealth platforms, RIAs, portfolio managers and eligible advisory firms with a regulated India wealth/advisory proposition.

✓ Legacy of Excellence in Channelising India’s Water Resources
Who Can Apply

Eligible institutions and organisations with a sustained, measurable contribution to channelising India’s water resources through responsible investment, financing or infrastructure initiatives.

Brand & Market Positioning

India-focused fintech and wealthtech brands demonstrating investor trust and education leadership.

✓ Emerging WealthTech Brand of the Year
Who Can Apply

India-focused wealthtech/investment brands generally ≤7 years old or in a clearly documented scale-up phase; regulated/eligible where required.

✓ Most Trusted Fintech Brand of the Year
Who Can Apply

India-focused fintech brands (broking, wealth, payments, insurtech, or lending) with a minimum of 2 years of operating history and demonstrable retail user base; SEBI/RBI/IRDAI registered or regulated where applicable; must demonstrate a consumer trust or investor education initiative in the nomination period.

✓ Best D2C Investment Platform
Who Can Apply

India-focused, app-first investment platforms offering direct (non-advisor-mediated) access to equities, mutual funds, bonds, gold, or alternative assets; generally ≤7 years old or in a clearly documented scale-up phase; SEBI-registered or operating under a regulated partner where required; platform must be live and transacting in the nomination period.
